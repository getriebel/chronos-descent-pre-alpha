The Premise

For centuries, humanity lived in the Golden Age—an era of unbroken health and peace, free of poverty or war. Yet after ages of abundance, dark clouds gathered over the earth. The elderly grew frail, creeping droughts withered the land, and the age of peace shattered into sickness and strife.
Then came the day the eldest of all—those who had lived beyond ten decades—vanished without a trace. One year later, no one remained on earth who stood within a single year of their hundredth winter.
Into this despair stepped a divine traveler: Hermes, herald of the Olympian gods. He revealed the truth behind the vanishing years—Chronos, Lord of Time and fallen ruler of the Titans, cast by Olympus into the deepest abyss of the Underworld. The plagues, droughts, and wars were the tremors of his awakening. Even in chains, Hermes warned, Chronos would continue to erase one generation of mortals each year, feasting upon their souls to regain his strength.
Olympus charged humanity with a desperate task: descend into the Underworld and slay Chronos before his count reaches zero. And so, year after year, humanity sends forth a nearly hopeless expedition of warriors doomed by the coming harvest—praying to sever the cycle before time itself runs out.
